# Colony observability

## Purpose

Make the consequences of player policy understandable through worker, building, and stockpile inspection rather than hidden scheduler behavior.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Explain goal and job state
Inspection data SHALL expose desired versus actual quantities, compatible in-flight work, job state, assignment, priority, and structured blocking reasons for the selected relevant object. Explanations SHALL identify their authoritative tick or revision.

#### Scenario: Workshop is waiting for material
- **WHEN** an inspector requests a workshop whose production is blocked by missing input
- **THEN** its data identifies the missing input and associated supply goal, available quantity, incoming commitments, and relevant job status without claiming production is underway

### Requirement: Trace custom-command provenance
Accepted goals and derived jobs SHALL retain the active policy revision, goal key, and source locations needed to trace a custom-command invocation to its declarations. Later edits SHALL NOT relabel old committed work as originating from unrelated new source.

#### Scenario: Inspect a helper-generated goal
- **WHEN** a player inspects work generated by a custom supply_workshop command
- **THEN** inspection identifies the goal and originating revision and offers both the invocation and the defining operation's source locations

### Requirement: Observe a bottleneck repair
The integrated game SHALL support diagnosing and repairing one policy-induced supply bottleneck without resetting the world, with the change visible in authoritative inspection and worker activity.

#### Scenario: Observe, revise, and recover
- **WHEN** the player approaches a workshop starved by a competing stock goal, inspects its shortage and policy provenance, and applies a policy that prioritizes its supply
- **THEN** compatible progress is retained, workers visibly carry the required supplies, inspection reports the changed commitments, and workshop production resumes
